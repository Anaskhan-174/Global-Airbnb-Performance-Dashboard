# 🌍 Global Airbnb Performance Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to analyze Airbnb listing performance, pricing, host performance, customer reviews, and market trends across major global cities.

## 📊 Dashboard Analysis

### 1️⃣ General Overview
- Monitor total listings, cities, hosts, property types, and reviews.
- Compare listing distribution across major Airbnb markets.
- Analyze listing growth and changes over time.
- Compare property types including Entire Place, Hotel Room, Private Room, and Shared Room.

### 2️⃣ Performance & Pricing Analysis
This page uses bookmarks to switch between **Overall Review** and **Detailed Review** views.

- Compare market share and performance across major cities.
- Analyze average price by property type.
- Compare Superhost and non-Superhost performance.
- Explore pricing and listing characteristics across markets.

### 3️⃣ Reviews & Customer Feedback Analysis
- Compare guest ratings across Accuracy, Cleanliness, Communication, Location, and Value.
- Analyze review frequency and reviewer behavior.
- Identify seasonal patterns in monthly reviews.
- Examine host trust signals such as profile pictures and identity verification.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Airbnb hosts and business teams need to understand how **market, property type, pricing, host performance, customer reviews, and seasonality** influence platform performance.

This dashboard combines these dimensions into an interactive view to support market comparison, pricing decisions, host benchmarking, and customer-experience improvement.

## 🎯 Dashboard Goals

- Identify the strongest Airbnb markets.
- Understand listing growth and market maturity.
- Compare pricing across property types.
- Benchmark Superhosts against non-Superhosts.
- Identify rating strengths and weaknesses.
- Understand customer review frequency and seasonality.
- Highlight trust and profile-verification patterns among hosts.

## 🔍 Key Findings

| Finding | Business Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Paris, New York, and Sydney account for almost half of total listings and 59% of total reviews.** | A small group of major markets contributes a very large share of platform activity, making these markets strategically important. |
| **Paris has the highest number of listings and reviews.** | Paris is a leading market and deserves focused pricing, supply, and host-performance analysis. |
| **Hotel rooms have the highest average price among the displayed property types.** | Higher-priced inventory can create opportunities for premium positioning and revenue optimization. |
| **Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro are the overall best-rated cities, while Hong Kong and Istanbul are the lowest-rated among the displayed markets.** | Lower-rated markets provide clear opportunities for improving the guest experience. |
| **Cleanliness and value for money are generally the lowest-scoring rating dimensions.** | Hosts can focus on property cleanliness and perceived value to improve customer satisfaction. |
| **98.8% of customers wrote reviews three times or less.** | Most reviewers are low-frequency contributors, so repeat-review behavior is concentrated among a small group. |
| **Paris and Rome dominate review share from April to August.** | European summer travel creates a visible seasonal increase in review activity. |
| **New York shows increased review activity in November and December.** | Holiday-season travel creates an additional seasonal pattern in the New York market. |
| **Over two-thirds of hosts are fully verified, while nearly all hosts provide at least one trust signal.** | Host verification and profile trust signals are strong platform-level trust indicators. |

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- **Prioritize major markets:** Focus market-level pricing, supply, and host strategies on high-contribution cities such as Paris, New York, and Sydney.
- **Optimize premium inventory:** Evaluate pricing and positioning opportunities for higher-priced property types, especially hotel rooms.
- **Improve cleanliness and value:** Encourage hosts in weaker-rated markets to improve property standards and align pricing with guest expectations.
- **Target seasonal demand:** Align host promotions, pricing, and availability with high-review periods such as the European summer and New York holiday season.
- **Strengthen host trust:** Continue encouraging identity verification and complete host profiles to maintain customer confidence.
- **Benchmark weaker markets:** Use high-performing markets such as Mexico City and Rio de Janeiro as reference points for customer-experience improvements.

## 📈 Business Impact

- **Market prioritization:** Identify markets contributing the largest share of listings and reviews.
- **Pricing strategy:** Compare average prices across property types and markets.
- **Customer experience:** Identify rating dimensions and markets requiring improvement.
- **Seasonality planning:** Support demand-aware pricing and availability decisions.
- **Host management:** Compare host performance and trust signals.
